# Qur'an dataset

No Qur'an text or font is bundled at this stage.

The intended primary source is the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex developer platform:
https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/quran-dev/

Before importing a dataset, record:
- Exact download URL and filename
- Edition and riwayah (Hafs)
- Release/version and publication date
- License/terms and required attribution
- SHA-256 checksum
- Row count and validation results

Required fields include a stable ayah identity, surah number/name, ayah number, Uthmani text, and Imla'i text. The Uthmani form is for display/replacement; the Imla'i form is for search.

Do not publish or redistribute a font or dataset until its specific terms have been reviewed. The repository's MIT license applies only to original source code.
